All about Wyverns

How would they fly?

 Similar to modern day bats wyverns would fly with two wings, and would stand on two hind legs. They would also need massive pectoral muscles in order to fly. Those muscles would be resonsible for supporting their body weight and help them maintaine balance during flight. In addition to massive pectorals, wyverns would also need extremely strong legs muscles in order to maintaine balance and support bodyweight while standing on their two hind legs.

Breathing fire

 As far as we know there is no creature on earth that can breathe fire like seen in tv shows and read in novels. However, it is highley likely that a creature such as this would use a mechanism similar to that of organisms that spit acid as a defense machanism. Firstly the creature must have a way to protect itself from its own defense mechanism. The mkost effective way for this to take place would be mucus secreation. The wyvern would have to have glands along its throat, mouth, and the lining of other internal organs that could possibly be breifly exposed to intense heat. Secondly, there would need to be gas. As the easiest way to generate enough fire tomcover a large surface are would be using gas. This means that the wyvern would need to have a mechanism that results in the production of highly combustable gas. This could be achieved by the wyvern maintaining a specific diet that results in the production or retention of this highly combustable gas. This gas would need to be kept in muscles that can act as gas chambers and can exit the body via pipes. In order to effectively contain this gas to a local area, such as these mucles, they would need a mechanism that allows these pipes to open and close when threatened, and adrenaline is released. There would also need to be a mechanism for combustion- something that would allow then gas to "catch on fire", and allow the wyvern to breath fire. One way this could work would be via some sort of beek made from organic material that results in a spark when friction is present. With a mechanism such as this, the illusion of fire breathing could be possible with the consistent and controlled factors. All things considered this would be a slwoer process than what is seen in media.
